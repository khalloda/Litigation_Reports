# 👥 Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## 🌟 Our Standards

### Positive Behavior
- ✅ Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward others
- ✅ Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- ✅ Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- ✅ Accepting responsibility and apologizing for mistakes
- ✅ Focusing on what is best for the overall community
- ✅ Showing appreciation for contributions

### Unacceptable Behavior
- ❌ Harassment, intimidation, or discrimination in any form
- ❌ Offensive, derogatory, or discriminatory comments or jokes
- ❌ Personal attacks or inflammatory language
- ❌ Publishing private information without consent
- ❌ Sustained disruption of discussions or events
- ❌ Inappropriate physical contact or sexual attention
- ❌ Advocating for or encouraging any of the above behaviors

## 🎯 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:
- GitHub repositories and issues
- Pull requests and code reviews
- Discussions and documentation
- Project meetings and events
- Social media and communication channels
- Email and direct messaging

It also applies when representing the project in external spaces.

## 🛡️ Enforcement

### Responsibilities
Community leaders are responsible for:
- Clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior
- Taking appropriate and fair corrective action
- Maintaining confidentiality when addressing reports
- Communicating decisions clearly and respectfully

### Reporting
If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior:
1. **Document the incident** with details, timestamps, and context
2. **Report to community leaders** at conduct@litigation-system.com
3. **Use GitHub's reporting features** for platform-specific issues
4. **Contact project maintainers** directly if needed

### Investigation Process
1. **Report Received**: Acknowledgment within 24 hours
2. **Initial Assessment**: Determine if immediate action needed
3. **Investigation**: Gather facts and context (3-7 days)
4. **Resolution**: Determine appropriate response
5. **Follow-up**: Communicate outcome to involved parties
6. **Documentation**: Record incident for future reference

## ⚖️ Enforcement Guidelines

### Correction Levels
Community leaders will use these guidelines when determining consequences:

| Level | Examples | Typical Consequences |
|-------|----------|---------------------|
| **Warning** | Minor violations, first offense | Private written warning with clarification |
| **Temporary Ban** | Repeated violations, moderate offenses | 1-4 week ban from community spaces |
| **Permanent Ban** | Severe violations, harassment, threats | Permanent ban from all community spaces |
| **Legal Action** | Criminal behavior, threats of violence | Involvement of law enforcement |

### Factors Considered
- **Intent**: Was the behavior malicious or accidental?
- **Impact**: How severely were others affected?
- **History**: Has this person violated the code before?
- **Power Dynamics**: Was there an imbalance of power?
- **Context**: What was the situation and environment?

## 📋 Specific Policies

### Online Communication
- Use clear, professional language
- Avoid sarcasm in technical discussions
- Give people the benefit of the doubt
- Assume good intent unless evidence shows otherwise
- Use inclusive language (they/them unless specified)

### Code Reviews
- Focus on the code, not the person
- Provide constructive, actionable feedback
- Accept feedback gracefully
- Use "I" statements ("I think this could be improved by...")
- Suggest alternatives rather than demanding changes

### Meetings and Events
- Respect time limits and agendas
- Allow equal participation opportunities
- Use inclusive presentation practices
- Provide accessibility accommodations
- Follow up on action items

### Social Media
- Attribute content properly
- Correct misinformation promptly
- Avoid engaging with bad-faith actors
- Represent the project positively
- Disclose your affiliation when relevant
